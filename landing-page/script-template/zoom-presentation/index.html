Zoom Presentation Script Template

Estimated delivery duration: 10–15 minutes · Markdown-ready for teleprompters

# Presentation Title --- ## OPEN --- - Welcome everyone. Wait 15s for late arrivals. - [Hook line]: "Today we are solving one specific bottleneck..." - Goal of today: [Outcome] ## SECTION 1: THE CURRENT STATE --- - Where we are today: [Metric] - The challenge: [Pain point] - (Pause for reaction) ## SECTION 2: PROPOSED SOLUTION --- - Transition: "So how do we fix this?" - Point A: [Logic] - Point B: [Data] ## SECTION 3: THE IMPACT --- - After implementation: [Future state] - ROI estimate: [Number] ## CLOSE --- - Next steps: [Action plan] - "Does anyone have questions about the timeline?"

How to use this template with Glance

1. Click Copy Markdown Template above.

2. Open Glance on your Mac or Windows PC and paste the script directly into the editor.

3. Glance will automatically detect Markdown headings (## OPEN, ## SECTION 1) so you can jump to specific slides or sections during unscripted Q&A using hotkeys.
