Teleprompter Overlay for Microsoft Teams

Deliver flawless executive updates, PowerPoint presentations, and client demos on Teams without looking away from your camera.

The challenge with Microsoft Teams meetings

During important Microsoft Teams calls — whether presenting quarterly results, sharing a PowerPoint deck, or demoing software to enterprise clients — keeping your gaze directed at the camera is crucial for establishing authority and trust.

However, referencing split-screen notes or presenter view monitors forces your eyes down or off to the side, breaking visual connection with colleagues and stakeholders.

How Glance solves it on Teams

Glance creates a discreet, semi-transparent teleprompter window positioned directly above your Microsoft Teams call interface, placed right beside your computer's webcam lens.

  • Always-on-top overlay: Sits cleanly over Microsoft Teams desktop or web app.
  • PowerPoint compatibility: Keep your presentation cues visible while running PowerPoint in full-screen slide show or Presenter view.
  • Section jumps & Markdown headings: Easily switch between agenda points during unscripted Q&A sessions.
  • Voice-aware auto-pausing: Scrolling pauses automatically when you stop to take questions or listen to attendees.

Privacy & Security for corporate teams

Enterprise professionals choose Glance because of its local-first security architecture:

  • No account or cloud sync: All scripts stay 100% on your local machine.
  • Offline execution: Works completely offline after initial one-time device license activation.
  • No recording capabilities: Glance does not capture, store, or transmit screen, audio, or video data.
