Teleprompter Overlay for Video Job Interviews

Keep your resume metrics, STAR method stories, and interview questions anchored right beside your camera without getting caught looking away.

Ace remote interviews with natural eye contact

During remote job interviews on Zoom, Microsoft Teams, or Google Meet, eye contact conveys confidence, authenticity, and leadership. When you break eye contact to look down at physical notes or glance sideways at a second monitor, interviewers perceive hesitation or lack of preparation.

However, memorizing dozens of exact numbers (revenue percentages, team sizes, project deadlines, and architectural decisions) is stressful. Glance solves this by placing a subtle, transparent overlay directly beside your camera lens, keeping your key memory anchors in your natural line of sight.

STAR Framework Anchors

Organize Situation, Task, Action, and Result bullets with Markdown headings for instantaneous access during behavioral questions.

Voice-Aware Auto-Pause

Scrolling pauses automatically while the hiring manager asks questions, resuming the moment you begin your response.

Stealth & Screen Share Safe

Glance is an overlay that sits on top of your local screen. When asked to share a browser tab or code IDE, Glance is completely invisible to interviewers.

100% Local-First Privacy

Your resume, confidential past company details, and interview questions remain strictly on your machine. No accounts, no cloud sync.

How candidates structure STAR responses in Glance

The golden rule for interview prompting is simple: do not write full paragraphs. Write concise cue bullets that trigger your memory while allowing you to speak authentically:

# 1. "Tell Me About Yourself" (90s Elevator Pitch) - Background: 6 yrs leading distributed engineering teams - Core superpower: Bridge high-scale architecture with product velocity - Why this role: Excited about Glance's local-first privacy architecture # 2. Behavioral: Conflict with Product Manager - Situation: PM wanted feature X in Q2, but database migration was critical - Task: Align priorities without sacrificing Q3 roadmap delivery - Action: Built quantified risk matrix & proposed 2-week staged migration - Result: Delivered on time with zero downtime; adopted as team standard # 3. Behavioral: High-Stakes Deadline - Situation: Enterprise client launch at risk due to API rate limit bottlenecks - Action: Implemented Redis caching layer & refactored batch webhook handling - Result: Cut API latency by 68%; closed $420k ARR renewal # 4. Questions for the Interviewer - What does success look like in the first 90 days for this role? - How does the team balance technical debt against fast feature delivery?

Frequently Asked Questions

Will my interviewer know I am using Glance?

No. Glance positions your notes within centimeters of your webcam lens. Because you are glancing at bullet cues rather than reading full paragraphs, your eyes stay focused on the camera with natural conversational cadence.

Does Glance work with Google Meet, Zoom, and Teams?

Yes. Glance is an always-on-top desktop application that floats independently above Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Google Meet, or browser-based interview platforms.

What if I am asked to live code or share my screen?

Glance only appears on your physical screen. When you share an application window (such as VS Code or Chrome), Glance is invisible to anyone viewing the shared screen.

Do I need an active internet connection?

No. After a one-time license verification on your device, Glance runs entirely offline without any internet connection.
