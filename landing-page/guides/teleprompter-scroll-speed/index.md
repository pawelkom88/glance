# Mastering Teleprompter Scroll Speed and Pacing

The most terrifying sensation for any teleprompter user is "chasing the text." If the prompter moves faster than you speak, you start tripping over words and racing your breath. If it moves too slowly, you are forced into unnatural, stilted pauses while waiting for the next line to scroll into view.

Controlling your scroll speed isn't just about setting a slider; it's about matching speech psychology to screen mechanics.

## Speaking Speed Benchmarks (WPM)

| Context | Target WPM | Characteristics |
|---|---|---|
| **Conversational Video Call** | 120 – 140 WPM | Relaxed, thoughtful, allows natural interruptions and nodding. |
| **Product Demo / Sales Pitch** | 130 – 150 WPM | Energetic and confident; crisp articulation of product features. |
| **Keynote / Formal Presentation** | 110 – 130 WPM | Slower pace to let key ideas resonate; intentional rhetorical pauses. |
| **Fast Casual Talk (Danger Zone)** | 160+ WPM | Feels rushed; listeners struggle to absorb complex information. |

## 3 Techniques for Manual Pacing Control

### 1. Read From the Upper Third
Always keep your eyes focused on the line that is approximately one-third down from the top of the prompter window. Do not wait for text to hit the absolute top edge before reading it. This gives you a 2-to-3-second visual cushion to preview coming sentences.

### 2. Insert Visual Breath Marks
Format your script with visual breathing anchors. Use ellipsis (`...`) or bracketed stage directions (`[PAUSE 2s]`). When you see these cues, deliberately look directly into the camera lens, take a half-breath, and smile before speaking the next phrase.

### 3. Use Stepped Increments Over Continuous Speed
Continuous motorized scrolling requires your speaking rate to be robotic. Stepped keyboard navigation (such as tapping the *Down Arrow* or scroll wheel every paragraph) gives you complete rhythmic freedom.

## The Modern Alternative: Speechmatics-Powered Voice Sync

Rather than forcing human speech to match a mechanical motor speed, modern teleprompters invert the paradigm: **the teleprompter listens to your voice and scrolls in sync with you**.

With [Glance Voice Sync](https://atglance.app/guides/voice-sync-teleprompter/), speech recognition matches your spoken words against your script in real-time. If you pause to answer a question or take a sip of water, the text stops immediately. When you resume, it automatically advances.
