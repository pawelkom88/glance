Teleprompter vs. Notes App for Video Calls

Why traditional notes apps or dual monitors fail on Zoom calls — and how floating overlays keep your focus aligned.

The Friction of Dual Monitors & Separate Windows

Placing notes in Notion, Apple Notes, or Google Docs requires splitting your screen. When you look at your notes window on the side of your monitor, your head turns away from your camera, breaking personal connection with attendees.

Side-by-Side Comparison

Feature Standard Notes App Glance Teleprompter
Camera Proximity Off to the side or bottom Snapped directly under webcam lens
Eye Contact Breaks eye contact when reading Maintains continuous camera line
Scrolling Manual mouse drag Speech-matched auto-scroll & hotkeys
Screen Share Safety Risks showing in full screen shares Invisible in tab/window shares

Conclusion

For high-stakes video meetings like sales calls, interviews, and keynotes, a dedicated teleprompter overlay like Glance gives you the confidence of full notes without looking away from your camera.
